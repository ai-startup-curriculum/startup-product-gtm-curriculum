# Startup Product & GTM Curriculum

**Role level:** 30 (deep functional pillar — Startup family)
**Family:** Startup
**Status:** planned — modules and projects below are the planned scope authored from [`.aicg/curriculum-plan.json`](.aicg/curriculum-plan.json). Lessons and worked exemplars will be drafted by subsequent autonomous content cycles, oldest-gap first.

## Overview

The deep functional pillar for the **customer-facing branch** of the startup dependency graph — Customer Discovery → PMF → Positioning → ICP → Founder-Led Sales → Pricing → Sales Motion → Demand Gen → Retention & Expansion → GTM Metrics & First Hires. Covers the IDEA-stage problem-validation loop through the Series-A boundary where the founder-led motion transitions into the first repeatable revenue engine.

The ten modules move a GTM operator from a first-principles discovery interview (*is this problem real and who has it?*), through an empirical PMF measurement engine (*have we found a market that will pull us?*), through the strategic-narrative and positioning work that makes the product legible (*what shelf does the buyer put us on?*), through the founder-led sales motion that closes the first 20-30 customers by hand (*can I sell this myself?*), through pricing and packaging designed against willingness-to-pay (*what is the buyer's value math?*), through the sales-motion design that stages the first repeatable engine (*PLG, SDR-AE, or enterprise?*), through demand-generation channel selection (*which one channel do I bet the quarter on?*), through the retention / expansion / growth-loop work that makes the motion durable (*does NRR ≥ 100%?*), and finally through the GTM metrics + first-hire staging that turns a founder-led motion into a scalable team (*which hire when?*). Total planned commitment: **218 hours across 10 modules** + **84 hours across 3 projects** = **~302 hours**.

## Ownership rule

Following the project-wide ownership rule, this curriculum:

- **Owns** the customer-facing branch depth from IDEA-stage problem validation through the Series-A boundary — Customer Discovery, PMF measurement, Positioning, ICP, Founder-Led Sales, Pricing & Packaging, Sales Motion Design (PLG / SDR-AE / Enterprise), Demand Generation, Retention & Expansion, Growth Loops, and GTM Metrics & First-Hire staging.
- **Defers down** to [`startup-foundations`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-foundations) (level 10) for the mental model — the graph, the stages, the founder's operating loop, and the founder-numbers slice (runway, burn, growth read, default alive / default dead). This track *never* re-teaches those; it references back.
- **Defers sideways** to [`cpo-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/cpo-curriculum) for product-management depth (feature prioritisation frameworks, roadmap discipline, product-ops, product-analytics tooling — the *how* of shipping product, distinct from the *what* of finding a market); to [`startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum) for unit-economics deep-dive beyond the GTM operator view (cohort financial modelling, capital-allocation frameworks, LTV discount-rate math, and Series-A fundraising narratives); to [`startup-operations-governance-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-operations-governance-curriculum) for GTM-org hiring / comp / IC-plan / management depth (quota carrying-rep comp structures, sales-comp legal, employment contracts).
- **Defers sideways / up** to the role-pathway repos ([`founder-ceo-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/founder-ceo-curriculum), [`cpo-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/cpo-curriculum)) that inherit this curriculum at their declared coverage and add role-specific curated views — no fork, no duplication.

## Module summaries

### mod-101 — Customer Discovery and Problem Validation
Run the customer-development loop end-to-end — problem interviews, solution interviews, product interviews — with Mom-Test discipline (talk about the interviewee's life, ask about the past, ask why), translate the corpus into JTBD outcomes (Ulwick / Christensen), and hand-recruit the first ten interviewees in the 'do things that don't scale' tradition. Ship a discovery report on a real startup — 20+ interviews summarised into problem statement, top jobs, top forces, disqualifying signals, and three testable hypotheses.

### mod-102 — PMF Signal and Measurement
Distinguish anecdotal traction (loud customer, launch spike, viral tweet) from product-market fit (retention curves that flatten, pull-based demand). Run the Sean Ellis 'very disappointed' survey properly against active users, read cohort retention curves, and reproduce the Superhuman iteration engine as a PMF *engine*, not a one-shot measurement. Diagnose the four PMF failure modes (wrong market, wrong product, wrong distribution, wrong price) and route each to the right downstream module.

### mod-103 — Positioning and Messaging
Author a positioning statement using the April Dunford five-component frame (competitive alternatives, unique attributes, value, best-for characteristics, market category); ship a strategic-narrative deck in the Andy Raskin shape (name the shift, enemy, promised land, evidence, obstacles, tools); apply Moore's Crossing-the-Chasm beachhead frame to pick the first defensible segment. Diagnose the three Dunford failure modes on real product pages.

### mod-104 — ICP and Buyer Personas
Define an ICP precise enough for a first sales hire to qualify in / out on the first call — firmographic + behavioural criteria plus a disqualification checklist. Separate buyer, user, and champion personas. Score real inbound / outbound leads against the scorecard and separate ICP-fit from ACV-fit from timing-fit. Diagnose ICP drift (the founder pattern of taking any paying customer).

### mod-105 — Founder-Led Sales
Run Kazanjy's founding-sales stage model end-to-end from cold outreach through discovery, demo, proposal, close. Author and run a discovery call using SPICED or MEDDIC / MEDDPICC. Enforce next-step discipline on every conversation. Run CRM hygiene at a founder scale (stage, next step, dollar value, honest weekly forecast). Close the first 20-30 paying customers by hand. Diagnose the tell-tale signs it is time for the first sales hire.

### mod-106 — Pricing and Packaging
Run willingness-to-pay research (Van Westendorp, Gabor-Granger, Max-Diff) with the trade-offs each measurement carries. Design value-based pricing anchored to the buyer's economic outcome. Design good / better / best packaging with an anchor tier. Distinguish pricing metric (the strategic choice — per seat / transaction / GB / workflow) from price point (the number that tests). Run price experiments at a startup scale with cohort-level tracking. Defer deep unit-economics modelling to `startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum`.

### mod-107 — Sales Motion Design
Match the sales motion to the ICP + ACV combination — PLG / self-serve at low ACV, SDR-AE inside-sales at mid-market ACV, enterprise MEDDPICC at high ACV. Design a PLG motion around activation event, PQL definition, in-product upgrade prompts. Design an outbound motion in the Predictable Revenue tradition. Design an enterprise motion around MEDDIC / MEDDPICC qualification. Thread the qualification framework through every CRM stage transition. Diagnose motion mismatches (PLG on enterprise contracts; enterprise ceremony on a developer product).

### mod-108 — Demand Generation and Channels
Pick one primary + one experimental channel per stage — founder-led outbound, inbound content + SEO, community, paid, partnerships — and reject the anti-pattern of running six half-funded channels. Diagnose channel-market fit (ICP-fit leads at a CAC that survives LTV / payback) before scaling spend. Design inbound topic strategy + publishing cadence, outbound cadence design, community motion, and paid at pre-Series-A with attribution honesty. Recognise the Andrew-Chen 'law of shitty click-throughs' as the founder-level channel-experimentation cadence discipline.

### mod-109 — Retention, Expansion, and Growth Loops
Read cohort retention curves as the primary PMF durability signal. Distinguish GRR from NRR and understand why NRR ≥ 100% at Series-A is the modern benchmark for a scalable SaaS motion. Design an expansion motion (seat, cross-sell, usage upgrade) as a first-order revenue lever. Replace the AARRR funnel with growth loops (Balfour / Reforge) where the loop applies. Identify the magic moment (the action a retained user takes in the first N days) and design an activation programme. Diagnose voluntary vs. involuntary churn separately.

### mod-110 — GTM Metrics and First Hires
Read CAC (fully-loaded, per-channel), LTV (cohort-based), payback, magic number, sales efficiency, funnel conversion, cohort retention, NRR against OpenView / SaaStr benchmarks by ARR band and motion type. Author a weekly + monthly GTM metrics one-pager for founder and board. Stage the first three GTM hires — founder-led sales through PMF, first AE after a repeatable script, first product marketer / demand-gen lead after AE is closing at target, first sales manager only after ≥3 AEs — and diagnose the wrong-first-hire pattern (VP Sales before PMF). Defer GTM-org comp / IC-plan / management depth to `startup-operations-governance-curriculum`.

Each project ships a portfolio-grade README under [`projects/`](projects/), a filled exemplar under [`exemplars/`](exemplars/) (discovery report, PMF scorecard, positioning document + narrative deck, ICP scorecard, founder-sales pack, pricing pack, motion-design doc, demand-gen plan, retention + expansion scorecard, GTM one-pager + first-hire staging plan), and an explicit rubric covering discovery interview quality, PMF measurement rigor, positioning defensibility against named alternatives, ICP precision, founder-led sales next-step discipline, pricing derivation from willingness-to-pay, motion-mismatch diagnosis, channel-market-fit judgement, retention-curve interpretation, NRR / GRR calculation, first-hire-staging defensibility, and honest weekly forecast vs. actuals.
