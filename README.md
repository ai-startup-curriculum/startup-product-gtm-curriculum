Deep functional pillar for the customer-facing branch of the startup dependency graph — **Customer Discovery → PMF → Positioning → ICP → Founder-Led Sales → Pricing → Sales Motion → Demand Gen → Retention & Expansion → GTM Metrics & First Hires**. Covers IDEA-stage problem validation through the Series-A boundary where the founder-led motion transitions into the first repeatable revenue engine.

> **Status**: planned. Curriculum shape authored in [`.aicg/curriculum-plan.json`](.aicg/curriculum-plan.json); requirements catalog in [`.aicg/job-requirements.json`](.aicg/job-requirements.json); role overview in [`CURRICULUM.md`](CURRICULUM.md). Lessons and worked exemplars will be drafted by subsequent autonomous content cycles.

**Level 30 · Startup family · ~302 hours (218h across 10 modules + 84h across 3 projects)**
