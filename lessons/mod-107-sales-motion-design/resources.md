# Resources — mod-107 Sales Motion Design

Citable references the module is grounded in. Primary sources and canonical practitioner texts first; adjacent operational writing second. Every chapter cites at least one item on this list; every module-level claim about a named framework (PLG, Predictable Revenue, Sales Acceleration Formula, MEDDIC / MEDDPICC, SPICED, PQL, activation event) traces back to a primary source below.

## SDR-AE inside sales — the Predictable Revenue tradition (Chapter 3)

- **Aaron Ross and Marylou Tyler — *Predictable Revenue: Turn Your Business Into a Sales Machine with the $100 Million Best Practices of Salesforce.com* (PebbleStorm Press, 2011).** The founding text of the modern SDR-AE motion — codifies the role split (SDR / AE), the outbound-cadence discipline, and the target-account list methodology. Every mid-market inside-sales motion built since draws on Ross's frame. Book page: [https://predictablerevenue.com/predictable-revenue](https://predictablerevenue.com/predictable-revenue).
- **Mark Roberge — *The Sales Acceleration Formula: Using Data, Technology, and Inbound Selling to Go from $0 to $100 Million* (Wiley, 2015).** Written from Roberge's time as SVP of Sales at HubSpot. Adds the hire-and-scale discipline (right salesperson, right sales process, right demand, right technology) and the numeric rigour (ratios, quotas, ramp) that most modern sales orgs still tune against. Wiley: [https://www.wiley.com/en-us/The+Sales+Acceleration+Formula%3A+Using+Data%2C+Technology%2C+and+Inbound+Selling+to+go+from+%240+to+%24100+Million-p-9781119047070](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/The+Sales+Acceleration+Formula%3A+Using+Data%2C+Technology%2C+and+Inbound+Selling+to+go+from+%240+to+%24100+Million-p-9781119047070).
- **Trish Bertuzzi — *The Sales Development Playbook: Build Repeatable Pipeline and Accelerate Growth with Inside Sales* (Moore Lange Publishing, 2016).** The SDR-specific companion to *Predictable Revenue* — covers SDR hiring, ramp, cadence design, target-account list construction, and hand-off criteria in detail. Book page: [https://bridgegroupinc.com/sales-development-playbook](https://bridgegroupinc.com/sales-development-playbook).
- **Bridge Group — Inside Sales benchmark reports.** [https://bridgegroupinc.com/research](https://bridgegroupinc.com/research). Annual benchmark data on SDR / AE ratios, quotas, OTE, ramp time, and attainment — the reference set Chapter 3's benchmark numbers should be pinned against.
- **Peter Kazanjy — *Founding Sales: The Early-Stage Go-to-Market Handbook* (Modern Sales Pros / Founding Sales, 2020; ongoing revisions).** The seed-to-Series-A founder-led-sales-through-first-hires text; overlaps with mod-105 and Chapter 3's founder-as-SDR-hybrid discussion. Site + book: [https://www.foundingsales.com/](https://www.foundingsales.com/).
- **Winning by Design — SPICED framework, Bowtie funnel, revenue architecture research.** [https://winningbydesign.com/resources/](https://winningbydesign.com/resources/). Jacco van der Kooij's firm; the SPICED (Situation / Pain / Impact / Critical Event / Decision) framework Chapter 3 references (and Chapter 5's stage model draws on) originates in Winning by Design's revenue-architecture playbook.
- **Josh Braun — outbound-sales writing and podcast.** [https://joshbraun.com/](https://joshbraun.com/). Long-form writing on cold-outreach copy, cadence tuning, and the buyer-psychology half of outbound.
- **LinkedIn Sales Solutions — *State of Sales* reports.** [https://business.linkedin.com/sales-solutions/b2b-sales-strategy-trends](https://business.linkedin.com/sales-solutions/b2b-sales-strategy-trends). Annual benchmark data on sales productivity, cadence performance, and multi-channel outreach; the reference set for response-rate benchmarks.

## Enterprise sales — MEDDIC / MEDDPICC (Chapter 4)

- **John McMahon — *The Qualified Sales Leader: Proven Lessons from a Five Time CRO* (John McMahon Group, 2020).** The modern SaaS-era reference for MEDDIC / MEDDPICC discipline, pipeline reviews, and enterprise sales-management practice. Written from McMahon's career running enterprise sales at PTC, Ariba, BladeLogic, and BMC. Book page: [https://www.thequalifiedsalesleader.com/](https://www.thequalifiedsalesleader.com/).
- **Andy Whyte — *MEDDICC: The Ultimate Guide to Staying One Step Ahead in the Complex Sale* (independently published, 2020; second edition 2023).** The comprehensive practitioner guide to the MEDDIC / MEDDPICC framework — each letter unpacked in detail with real-world examples and coaching guidance. Site: [https://meddicc.com/](https://meddicc.com/).
- **MEDDIC Academy / MEDDPICC Academy — Jack Napoli and Darius Lahoutifard training organisation.** [https://meddic.academy/](https://meddic.academy/). Founded by Jack Napoli (co-creator of MEDDIC at PTC in the 1990s); ongoing training organisation that codifies the framework and licenses it to enterprise sales teams.
- **Force Management — MEDDPICC and Command of the Message writing.** [https://www.forcemanagement.com/](https://www.forcemanagement.com/). Enterprise sales training firm; long body of published writing on MEDDPICC in practice, Command of the Message positioning, and enterprise pipeline reviews.
- **Sales Assembly — enterprise sales research and community writing.** [https://salesassembly.com/](https://salesassembly.com/). Peer community for enterprise-SaaS sales leaders; publishes research on SE / AE ratios, enterprise deal-cycle patterns, and hiring benchmarks.
- **Bob Perkins — AA-ISP (American Association of Inside Sales Professionals) and enterprise-sales community writing.** [https://www.aa-isp.org/](https://www.aa-isp.org/). Long-standing industry association; benchmark reports on enterprise SDR / AE / SE structures.

## Suggested reading order for this module

If starting cold:

1. Read [Aaron Ross and Marylou Tyler — *Predictable Revenue*](https://predictablerevenue.com/predictable-revenue) end-to-end (~5 hr).
2. Read [Mark Roberge — *The Sales Acceleration Formula*](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/The+Sales+Acceleration+Formula%3A+Using+Data%2C+Technology%2C+and+Inbound+Selling+to+go+from+%240+to+%24100+Million-p-9781119047070) end-to-end (~6 hr).
3. Read [Wes Bush — *Product-Led Growth*](https://productled.com/book) end-to-end (~5 hr).
4. Read [John McMahon — *The Qualified Sales Leader*](https://www.thequalifiedsalesleader.com/) end-to-end (~6 hr).
5. Read [Andy Whyte — *MEDDICC*](https://meddicc.com/) end-to-end (~4 hr).
6. Read [Trish Bertuzzi — *The Sales Development Playbook*](https://bridgegroupinc.com/sales-development-playbook) chapters on cadence design and hand-off criteria (~3 hr).
7. Read [Peter Kazanjy — *Founding Sales*](https://www.foundingsales.com/) chapters on the founder-led-to-first-hire transition (~3 hr; overlaps with mod-105).
8. Skim [OpenView PLG Index](https://openviewpartners.com/blog/) and [Kyle Poyar's Growth Unhinged](https://www.growthunhinged.com/) archive for benchmark and operating updates (~2 hr).
9. Skim [Winning by Design's Bowtie / SPICED / revenue-architecture materials](https://winningbydesign.com/resources/) (~2 hr).
10. Skim [Bridge Group Inside Sales benchmark](https://bridgegroupinc.com/research) reports for current-year SDR / AE ratios and quotas (~1 hr).

Total: ~37 hours of prerequisite reading before authoring a defensible motion pack against the exercises. Most founders will not read all of this before starting the module; the suggested order is what a rigorous reader would work through, and any subset is better than none. Chapters 1-6 of this module are written assuming the reader has *not* completed this reading and can therefore be used as an accelerated entry into the material.

## Provenance

This module cites primary sources first — Aaron Ross's *Predictable Revenue* for the SDR-AE role split, Mark Roberge's *Sales Acceleration Formula* for the hire-and-scale discipline, Wes Bush's *Product-Led Growth* and OpenView's PLG research for the PLG motion frame, John McMahon's *The Qualified Sales Leader* and Andy Whyte's *MEDDICC* for the MEDDIC / MEDDPICC discipline, Winning by Design's Bowtie / SPICED work for the revenue-architecture frame that Chapter 5's stage model draws on, and Trish Bertuzzi's *The Sales Development Playbook* for the SDR-specific hiring and cadence content. Practitioner-writing links (Christoph Janz, David Skok, Kyle Poyar, Peter Kazanjy, Casey Winters, Josh Braun, Tomasz Tunguz, Jason Lemkin, First Round Review, Lenny Rachitsky) are included where they operationalise the primary sources cleanly and are broadly considered canonical within the B2B SaaS GTM practitioner community. Tool links (HubSpot, Salesforce, Attio, Close, Outreach, SalesLoft, Apollo, Gong, Amplitude, Segment, Pendo, Stripe, etc.) are the actual products referenced in the chapters. If a factual claim in a chapter cannot be traced to one of the sources on this page (or to another primary source cited inline), it is a bug — please flag it via a `needs-research` comment against the offending chapter.
