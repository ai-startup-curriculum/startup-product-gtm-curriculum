# mod-107 — Sales Motion Design (PLG, Self-Serve, Inside-Sales, Enterprise)

**Stage:** SEED
**Pillar:** GTM
**Hours:** 22 (6 lecture chapters + 6 exercises + 1 lab)
**Track:** [Startup Product & GTM](../../CURRICULUM.md)

> *The motion is a derivation from the ICP and the ACV. Pick the one the matrix cell demands, not the one your career history prefers.*

## What this module trains

Derive the primary GTM motion from the **ICP** (mod-104) and the **ACV** (mod-106) rather than from founder preference, investor fashion, or past-company habit — locate the deal on a matrix of **ACV band × self-service willingness**, pick the archetype the cell demands (**PLG / self-serve** at low ACV + high self-service, **SDR-AE inside sales** at mid-market ACV, **enterprise MEDDPICC** at high ACV + buying-committee complexity), rule out the mismatches explicitly, and defend the choice against a board member or a first-sales-hire candidate. Design the primary motion in operating detail: for PLG, the five load-bearing pieces (activation event, PQL definition, in-product upgrade prompts, sales-assist trigger, expansion trigger) in the [Wes Bush / OpenView](https://productled.com/book) tradition; for inside sales, the SDR-AE split, the multi-channel cadence, the response-rate benchmarks, and the hand-off criteria in the [Aaron Ross *Predictable Revenue*](https://predictablerevenue.com/predictable-revenue) / [Mark Roberge *Sales Acceleration Formula*](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/The+Sales+Acceleration+Formula%3A+Using+Data%2C+Technology%2C+and+Inbound+Selling+to+go+from+%240+to+%24100+Million-p-9781119047070) tradition; for enterprise, the full **MEDDIC / MEDDPICC** discipline — Metrics, Economic buyer, Decision criteria, Decision process, Identify pain, Champion, Paper process, Competition — threaded through the buying committee and the paper process in the [John McMahon](https://www.thequalifiedsalesleader.com/) / [Andy Whyte](https://meddicc.com/) tradition. Thread the qualification framework (SPICED for mid-market, MEDDPICC for enterprise) through **every CRM stage transition** so no deal advances on gut feel — every stage has an entry criterion, an exit criterion, a required artifact, a MEDDPICC / SPICED coverage state, and a forecast probability calibrated against the team's own historical conversion. Diagnose motion mismatches using the **five-step teardown** — locate on the Chapter 1 matrix, read the CRM signals, score the three symptom families (unit-economic, funnel-shape, team-and-narrative), match to one of the four canonical mismatches (enterprise ceremony on a self-serve product; self-serve on a $100K contract; mid-market SDR-AE on a bottoms-up developer product; sub-scale CAC on any motion), and prescribe the fix with motion + pack + roles + sequencing.

The failure mode this module exists to catch: **the founder installs a sales motion from career habit or investor advice without deriving it from the ICP + ACV combination, spends two quarters tuning the wrong motion against the wrong market, misreads a motion mismatch as an execution problem and reaches for in-motion levers (hire another SDR, coach the AE harder, buy a better cadence tool) — and burns 6-18 months of runway on a motion that never fit, often in parallel with half-running two or three other motions none of which is fully invested in either.**

## Learning objectives

- Match the sales motion to the ICP + ACV combination — PLG / self-serve at low ACV and high self-service willingness, SDR-AE inside sales at mid-market ACV, enterprise MEDDPICC at high ACV — and know why forcing the wrong motion (enterprise ceremony on a self-serve product; self-serve on a $100K contract; SDR-AE on a bottoms-up developer product) breaks GTM.
- Design a PLG motion — activation event derived from cohort analysis, account-level PQL definition, in-product upgrade prompts (paywall / value-realisation / social-proof / expansion archetypes), sales-assist trigger with SLA, expansion trigger (seat / usage / feature / cross-product) — using the Wes Bush and OpenView playbooks.
- Design an SDR-AE outbound motion in the Predictable Revenue tradition — the SDR-AE split, SDR / AE ratios by ACV band, multi-channel multi-touch cadence design, response-rate benchmarks, hand-off criteria with a 60-80% hand-off-accepted target — and know when the founder is the SDR-AE-hybrid and when to hire the split (AE first, SDR after, sales manager not before ≥3 AEs).
- Design an enterprise motion around MEDDIC / MEDDPICC qualification — Metrics, Economic buyer, Decision criteria, Decision process, Identify pain, Champion, plus Paper process and Competition — map the buying committee (economic buyer / champion / technical evaluator / user / procurement / legal / security / finance / executive sponsor / blockers), thread the discipline through every stage, run the SE / POC / paper-process rhythms the deal shape requires.
- Thread the qualification framework through the CRM — a 5-7 stage buyer-commitment-based model, defined entry / exit criteria per stage, a required artifact per stage, a MEDDPICC or SPICED coverage panel per opportunity, calibrated forecast probabilities, loss-reason coding, and weekly pipeline hygiene so no deal advances on gut feel.
- Diagnose motion mismatches — enterprise ceremony on a self-serve product; self-serve on a $100K contract; mid-market SDR-AE on a bottoms-up developer product; sub-scale CAC / payback on any motion — using the three symptom families (unit-economic / funnel-shape / team-and-narrative) and the five-step teardown, and prescribe the fix with motion + pricing-pack + role-reallocation + sequencing.
- Ship a motion design doc — the matrix-located primary motion, the layer(s), the ruled-out alternatives, the operating detail (PLG five-piece or SDR-AE roles-cadence-handoff or enterprise MEDDPICC-stages-rhythm), the CRM stage model with artifact-per-stage, and the quarterly teardown cadence — the artifact the first-sales-hire inherits and the board reads.
- Recognise the boundary — motion design lives here; **retention and expansion** motion depth defers to [mod-109](../mod-109-retention-expansion-and-growth-loops); **demand creation** (content, community, paid, partnerships) defers to [mod-108](../mod-108-demand-generation-and-channels); **first-hire sequencing + GTM metrics** org-scale discipline defers to [mod-110](../mod-110-gtm-metrics-and-first-hires); **unit-economics modelling for fundraising** defers up to [`startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum).

## How this module connects to the rest of the track

- **Fed by ← [mod-101](../mod-101-customer-discovery-and-problem-validation)** — the discovery corpus and pain articulation feed the pain-sizing conversation that MEDDIC's Identify Pain and PLG's activation-event rationale rest on. Motion design without discovery is motion design against a market the founder hasn't actually heard.
- **Fed by ← [mod-102](../mod-102-pmf-signal-and-measurement)** — the PMF cohort characterisation feeds the ICP-fit discipline that both the PLG PQL definition and the SDR-AE target-account list depend on. Motion design before PMF is motion design against a market the founder cannot characterise.
- **Fed by ← [mod-103](../mod-103-positioning-and-messaging)** — the positioning statement feeds the demo narrative, the outbound value hypothesis, and the champion-enablement collateral the enterprise motion needs. A motion built without positioning defaults to feature tours in every stage.
- **Fed by ← [mod-104](../mod-104-icp-and-buyer-personas)** — the ICP scorecard, the firmographic + behavioural + buyer / user / champion split, and the beachhead-segment selection are the first upstream input to Chapter 1's matrix derivation. A motion built without a scoreable ICP cannot defensibly answer "why this motion, not another?"
- **Fed by ← [mod-105](../mod-105-founder-led-sales)** — the founder-led-sales pack (discovery script, demo checklist, proposal template, next-step discipline, CRM stage definitions, win / loss pattern) is the raw material the SDR-AE motion (Chapter 3) and the enterprise motion (Chapter 4) are built on. Chapter 3's "when to hire the split" discussion is the direct successor to mod-105 Chapter 8's readiness diagnostic.
- **Fed by ← [mod-106](../mod-106-pricing-and-packaging)** — the pricing pack (primary ACV, pricing metric, tier structure) is the second upstream input to Chapter 1's matrix derivation. A motion built without a pricing pack has no ACV band to locate; a motion mismatch (Chapter 6) usually requires a joint pricing-pack change (mod-106 Chapter 6's grandfathering discipline is load-bearing on any motion pivot).
- **Feeds → [mod-108](../mod-108-demand-generation-and-channels)** — the motion's top-of-funnel requirement (PLG organic + referral + community; SDR-AE outbound target-account list; enterprise named-account outbound) is the entry criterion for mod-108's channel selection. mod-107 names what the motion needs; mod-108 designs the channel program that produces it.
- **Feeds → [mod-109](../mod-109-retention-expansion-and-growth-loops)** — Chapter 2's expansion-trigger section and Chapter 5's Land / CS stage point directly into mod-109. mod-107 owns acquisition-through-upgrade; mod-109 owns retention-through-expansion. NRR targets at Series A rest on expansion triggers designed here.
- **Feeds → [mod-110](../mod-110-gtm-metrics-and-first-hires)** — Chapter 3's AE / SDR / sales-manager / VP-Sales hiring sequence, Chapter 4's SE hiring timing, and Chapter 5's forecast / pipeline-review cadence are the direct inputs to mod-110's first-hire staging discipline. mod-107 names the motion; mod-110 operates the metrics and hiring sequence around it.
- **Feeds → [`project-101-first-thirty-paying-customers`](../../projects/project-101-first-thirty-paying-customers)** — the motion design doc is the operating frame the first-30-customer push runs on once the founder-led motion from mod-105 is giving way to the first scaled motion.
- **Defers up → [`startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum)** — Chapter 6's Family A (unit-economic) diagnostics name CAC / payback / margin at the diagnostic level; cohort DCF with discount rates, LTV modelling for the Series-A fundraising narrative, and valuation-multiple analysis defer up to the finance curriculum.
- **Defers up → [`startup-operations-governance-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-operations-governance-curriculum)** — Chapter 3's comp-plan sketch (base / variable / quota / accelerator / clawback) and Chapter 4's enterprise AE comp structure name the shape; detailed IC-plan modelling, quota-setting for org-scale teams, and sales-comp legal defer up to the operations / governance curriculum.
