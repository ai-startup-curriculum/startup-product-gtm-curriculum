# Resources — mod-102 PMF Signal and Measurement

Citable references the module is grounded in. Primary sources first; adjacent practitioner writing second. Every chapter cites at least one item on this list.

## Product-market fit — the definitional canon

- **Marc Andreessen — "The Only Thing That Matters" (2007).** The essay that took the term mainstream. Archived at the pmarchive: [https://pmarchive.com/guide_to_startups_part4.html](https://pmarchive.com/guide_to_startups_part4.html).
- **Andy Rachleff — "Product/Market Fit: What it really means, How to Measure it, and Where to find it".** The clearest single statement of the value-hypothesis vs. growth-hypothesis frame from the person who coined the phrase. [https://www.wealthfront.com/blog/product-market-fit/](https://www.wealthfront.com/blog/product-market-fit/). Related essays and interviews indexed on the Wealthfront blog: [https://www.wealthfront.com/blog/tag/andy-rachleff/](https://www.wealthfront.com/blog/tag/andy-rachleff/).
- **Andy Rachleff — Stanford Graduate School of Business talks and course materials.** Rachleff teaches a Stanford GSB course on entrepreneurship; several of his public talks re-state the PMF frame with additional case studies. Search "Andy Rachleff Stanford" for current listings.

## The Sean Ellis survey

- **Sean Ellis — "Using Product/Market Fit to Drive Sustainable Growth" (2009).** The original blog post proposing the survey and the 40% heuristic. Archived: [https://web.archive.org/web/20101025110359/http://startup-marketing.com/using-product-market-fit-to-drive-sustainable-growth/](https://web.archive.org/web/20101025110359/http://startup-marketing.com/using-product-market-fit-to-drive-sustainable-growth/).
- **pmfsurvey.com — Sean Ellis's tooling for running the survey.** Canonical question wording and template. [https://pmfsurvey.com/](https://pmfsurvey.com/).
- **Sean Ellis and Morgan Brown — *Hacking Growth* (2017).** The book that unpacks the survey as one instrument inside a broader growth-experimentation cadence. [https://www.hackingrowth.com/](https://www.hackingrowth.com/).
- **GrowthHackers.com** — Ellis's community; publishes ongoing case-study writing on the survey and adjacent experimentation. [https://growthhackers.com/](https://growthhackers.com/).

## The Superhuman iteration engine

- **Rahul Vohra — "How Superhuman Built an Engine to Find Product/Market Fit", First Round Review (2018).** The load-bearing operational essay for Chapter 5. Read end-to-end. [https://review.firstround.com/how-superhuman-built-an-engine-to-find-product-market-fit/](https://review.firstround.com/how-superhuman-built-an-engine-to-find-product-market-fit/).
- **Rahul Vohra — podcast interviews on the Superhuman engine.** The First Round Review essay is the primary reference; several podcast interviews expand on the four-step loop with additional case detail (search "Rahul Vohra product market fit podcast").
- **Superhuman blog and founder talks** — additional context on the product and the segment they targeted. [https://blog.superhuman.com/](https://blog.superhuman.com/).

## Retention curves and cohort analysis

- **Brian Balfour — "The Never Ending Road to Product/Market Fit".** Argues PMF is a system state, not a milestone; retention flattening is the durable signature. [https://brianbalfour.com/essays/product-market-fit](https://brianbalfour.com/essays/product-market-fit).
- **Brian Balfour — Four Fits framework essays.** Deeper treatment of Market-Product-Channel-Model fit; adjacent to the four-failure-mode frame this module teaches. [https://brianbalfour.com/four-fits-growth-framework](https://brianbalfour.com/four-fits-growth-framework).
- **Andrew Chen — "The Power User Curve" and related essays on retention.** Popularised the "smile" curve reading. [https://andrewchen.com/power-user-curve/](https://andrewchen.com/power-user-curve/); index of retention essays at [https://andrewchen.com/](https://andrewchen.com/).
- **Andrew Chen — "New data shows losing 80% of mobile users is normal".** The canonical practitioner reference on consumer mobile retention benchmarks. [https://andrewchen.com/new-data-shows-why-losing-80-of-your-mobile-users-is-normal-and-that-the-best-apps-do-much-better/](https://andrewchen.com/new-data-shows-why-losing-80-of-your-mobile-users-is-normal-and-that-the-best-apps-do-much-better/).
- **Andrew Chen — *The Cold Start Problem* (2021).** Book-length treatment of network-effects products, including deeper retention-curve reads. [https://www.coldstart.com/](https://www.coldstart.com/).
- **Amplitude — "The Amplitude Guide to Retention".** Practitioner reference for cohort retention analytics setup and reading. [https://amplitude.com/blog/product-retention](https://amplitude.com/blog/product-retention).
- **Amplitude — North Star Playbook.** Framework for choosing the "active" event that anchors retention reads. [https://amplitude.com/north-star](https://amplitude.com/north-star).
- **Mixpanel — Retention analysis docs.** Reference for cohort retention reports and the definition tradeoffs. [https://docs.mixpanel.com/docs/reports/retention](https://docs.mixpanel.com/docs/reports/retention).
- **Reforge — blog writing on retention and PMF.** Ongoing practitioner writing from Balfour's team. [https://www.reforge.com/blog](https://www.reforge.com/blog).

## The four PMF failure modes and adjacent diagnostic frameworks

- **Brian Balfour — Four Fits framework** (see above) — the closest published articulation of the market/product/channel/model separation the module operationalises as the four failure modes.
- **Marc Andreessen — "The Only Thing That Matters"** (see above) — Andreessen's original taxonomy of failure modes ("bad team, bad product, bad market") is a precursor to the four-mode frame.
- **Jason Cohen — "A smart bear" archive.** Practitioner critique of common PMF misdiagnoses; useful for pattern-recognition on the "we don't have PMF, we have X" failure. [https://longform.asmartbear.com/](https://longform.asmartbear.com/).

## Suggested reading order for this module

If starting cold:

1. Read [Marc Andreessen — "The Only Thing That Matters"](https://pmarchive.com/guide_to_startups_part4.html) (~15 min).
2. Read [Andy Rachleff — "Product/Market Fit: What it really means"](https://www.wealthfront.com/blog/product-market-fit/) (~10 min).
3. Read [Sean Ellis — "Using Product/Market Fit to Drive Sustainable Growth" (archived)](https://web.archive.org/web/20101025110359/http://startup-marketing.com/using-product-market-fit-to-drive-sustainable-growth/) (~15 min).
4. Read [Rahul Vohra — "How Superhuman Built an Engine to Find Product/Market Fit"](https://review.firstround.com/how-superhuman-built-an-engine-to-find-product-market-fit/) end to end (~45 min).
5. Read [Brian Balfour — "The Never Ending Road to Product/Market Fit"](https://brianbalfour.com/essays/product-market-fit) (~20 min).
6. Skim [Amplitude — "The Amplitude Guide to Retention"](https://amplitude.com/blog/product-retention) for the analytics vocabulary (~30 min).
7. Skim [Andrew Chen — "The Power User Curve"](https://andrewchen.com/power-user-curve/) for the smile-curve shape (~15 min).

Total: ~2.5 hours of prerequisite reading before running the exercises against real (or realistic hypothetical) data.
