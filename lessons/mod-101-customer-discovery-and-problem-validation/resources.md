# Resources — mod-101 Customer Discovery and Problem Validation

Citable references the module is grounded in. Primary sources first; adjacent practitioner writing second. Every chapter cites at least one item on this list.

## Customer development — Steve Blank

- **Steve Blank — *The Four Steps to the Epiphany* (2005).** The founding text of the customer-development discipline. Stanford course PDF: [https://web.stanford.edu/group/e145/cgi-bin/spring/upload/handouts/Four_Steps.pdf](https://web.stanford.edu/group/e145/cgi-bin/spring/upload/handouts/Four_Steps.pdf).
- **Steve Blank — *The Startup Owner's Manual* (2012, with Bob Dorf).** The workbook expansion of the customer-development method. Author's own summary of chapter 1: [https://steveblank.com/2020/03/03/the-startup-owners-manual-1-a-step-by-step-guide-for-building-a-successful-company/](https://steveblank.com/2020/03/03/the-startup-owners-manual-1-a-step-by-step-guide-for-building-a-successful-company/).
- **Steve Blank — blog archive at [steveblank.com](https://steveblank.com/).** Ongoing writing on customer development, lean startup, and Stanford I-Corps.

## The Mom Test — Rob Fitzpatrick

- **Rob Fitzpatrick — *The Mom Test* (2013).** Book site with author's own summary and companion resources: [https://www.momtestbook.com/](https://www.momtestbook.com/).
- **Rob Fitzpatrick — *The Workshop Survival Guide* (2019, with Devin Hunt).** Companion book on running the workshop version of these interview conversations. [https://www.workshopsurvival.com/](https://www.workshopsurvival.com/).

## Jobs-to-be-Done — Ulwick, Christensen, Moesta

- **Anthony Ulwick — *What Customers Want* (2005).** The founding text of Outcome-Driven Innovation. Author's firm site with methodology overviews: [https://strategyn.com/jobs-to-be-done/](https://strategyn.com/jobs-to-be-done/).
- **Anthony Ulwick — "Turn Customer Input into Innovation", *Harvard Business Review* (2002).** [https://hbr.org/2002/01/turn-customer-input-into-innovation](https://hbr.org/2002/01/turn-customer-input-into-innovation).
- **Clayton Christensen — *Competing Against Luck* (2016, with Taddy Hall, Karen Dillon, David S. Duncan).** The canonical JTBD narrative treatment. [https://www.claytonchristensen.com/books/competing-against-luck/](https://www.claytonchristensen.com/books/competing-against-luck/).
- **Clayton Christensen — "Know Your Customers' 'Jobs to Be Done'", *Harvard Business Review* (2016).** [https://hbr.org/2016/09/know-your-customers-jobs-to-be-done](https://hbr.org/2016/09/know-your-customers-jobs-to-be-done).
- **Bob Moesta — *Demand-Side Sales 101* (2020).** The forces-of-progress framing applied to selling and switching. [https://www.rewiredgroup.com/books](https://www.rewiredgroup.com/books).
- **The Re-Wired Group** — Moesta's firm; publishes case-study writing on switch-interview methodology. [https://www.rewiredgroup.com/](https://www.rewiredgroup.com/).

## "Do things that don't scale" — Paul Graham and Y Combinator

- **Paul Graham — "Do Things That Don't Scale" (2013).** [https://paulgraham.com/ds.html](https://paulgraham.com/ds.html).
- **Paul Graham — "Startup = Growth" (2012).** [https://paulgraham.com/growth.html](https://paulgraham.com/growth.html). Anchored in mod-101 as the operating tradition even though the metric-side lives in mod-102 / mod-110.

## Suggested reading order for this module

If starting cold:

1. Skim [Paul Graham — "Do Things That Don't Scale"](https://paulgraham.com/ds.html) — the operating tradition (~15 min).
2. Read [Rob Fitzpatrick — *The Mom Test*](https://www.momtestbook.com/) chapters 1–3 — the interview discipline (~1 hr).
3. Read [Steve Blank — *The Four Steps to the Epiphany* Stanford PDF](https://web.stanford.edu/group/e145/cgi-bin/spring/upload/handouts/Four_Steps.pdf) chapters on customer discovery (~2 hrs).
4. Read [Clayton Christensen — "Know Your Customers' Jobs to Be Done" (HBR 2016)](https://hbr.org/2016/09/know-your-customers-jobs-to-be-done) — the JTBD narrative framing (~30 min).
5. Skim [Anthony Ulwick / Strategyn overview](https://strategyn.com/jobs-to-be-done/) for the ODI structural version (~30 min).

Total: ~4–5 hours of prerequisite reading before running any of the exercises against real interviewees.
