## Qualification methodologies — MEDDIC / MEDDPICC / SPICED / Challenger

The three-axis routing (Chapter 5) and the buyer / user / champion split (Chapter 4) are compatible with all four canonical qualification methodologies; the module explicitly names MEDDIC's "Economic Buyer" as the instrument catching the champion-without-authority trap.

- **MEDDIC / MEDDPICC.** Developed by Jack Napoli, Dick Dunkel, and John McMahon at PTC (Parametric Technology Corporation) in the 1990s during PTC's rapid enterprise-sales scale-up. The acronym: **M**etrics, **E**conomic Buyer, **D**ecision Criteria, **D**ecision Process, **I**dentify Pain, **C**hampion (MEDDIC); MEDDPICC extends with **P**aper Process and **C**ompetition.
  - **John McMahon — *The Qualified Sales Leader: Proven Lessons from a Five Time CRO* (2021).** The most comprehensive practitioner treatment; McMahon co-created MEDDIC. Amazon page: [https://www.amazon.com/Qualified-Sales-Leader-Proven-Lessons/dp/0578895064](https://www.amazon.com/Qualified-Sales-Leader-Proven-Lessons/dp/0578895064).
  - **MEDDICC (organisation) — Andy Whyte — *MEDDICC: The Ultimate Guide to Staying One Step Ahead in the Complex Sale* (2020).** Practitioner reference book with the full MEDDPICC treatment; supplementary resources at [https://meddicc.com/](https://meddicc.com/).
- **SPICED — Winning by Design.** Developed by Jacco van der Kooij's Winning by Design as a buyer-centered alternative to MEDDIC. Acronym: **S**ituation, **P**ain, **I**mpact, **C**ritical Event, **D**ecision. Free-content library including the SPICED cards and buyer-frame docs: [https://winningbydesign.com/resources/](https://winningbydesign.com/resources/).
  - **Jacco van der Kooij and Fernando Pizarro — *Blueprints for a SaaS Sales Organization* (2015; 3rd ed. 2018).** The Winning by Design methodology in book form. [https://www.amazon.com/Blueprints-SaaS-Sales-Organization-repeatable/dp/0996230122](https://www.amazon.com/Blueprints-SaaS-Sales-Organization-repeatable/dp/0996230122).
- **Challenger Sale / Challenger Customer.**
  - **Matthew Dixon and Brent Adamson — *The Challenger Sale: Taking Control of the Customer Conversation* (2011).** The CEB research on which sales reps outperform; the "Challenger" archetype. Publisher page: [https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/311570/the-challenger-sale-by-matthew-dixon-and-brent-adamson/](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/311570/the-challenger-sale-by-matthew-dixon-and-brent-adamson/).
  - **Brent Adamson, Matthew Dixon, Pat Spenner, and Nick Toman — *The Challenger Customer: Selling to the Hidden Influencer Who Can Multiply Your Results* (2015).** The mobilizer / talker / sceptic buyer-committee taxonomy — a more granular treatment of Chapter 4's champion split at enterprise ACVs. [https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/315928/the-challenger-customer-by-brent-adamson-matthew-dixon-pat-spenner-and-nick-toman/](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/315928/the-challenger-customer-by-brent-adamson-matthew-dixon-pat-spenner-and-nick-toman/).
- **Neil Rackham — *SPIN Selling* (1988).** The original research-anchored qualification methodology (Situation / Problem / Implication / Need-payoff). Precedes MEDDIC / SPICED and is the methodological ancestor of Chapter 5's discovery-question discipline. [https://www.spinsellingfieldbook.com/](https://www.spinsellingfieldbook.com/) (fieldbook site).

## Suggested reading order for this module

If starting cold:

1. Read [Geoffrey Moore — *Crossing the Chasm*](https://www.harpercollins.com/products/crossing-the-chasm-3rd-edition-geoffrey-a-moore) Introduction + Chapters 1-4 (~2 hr).
2. Read [April Dunford — *Sales Pitch*](https://www.aprildunford.com/sales-pitch) end-to-end (~2 hr).
3. Read [John McMahon — *The Qualified Sales Leader*](https://www.amazon.com/Qualified-Sales-Leader-Proven-Lessons/dp/0578895064) Parts 1-2 on MEDDIC and buying-committee mapping (~2 hr).
4. Skim [Mark Roberge — *The Sales Acceleration Formula*](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/The+Sales+Acceleration+Formula%3A+Using+Data%2C+Technology%2C+and+Inbound+Selling+to+go+from+%240+to+%24100+Million-p-9781119047070) Chapters 1-4 on ICP-driven repeatable motion (~1 hr).
5. Read [Rahul Vohra on Superhuman](https://review.firstround.com/how-superhuman-built-an-engine-to-find-product-market-fit/) for the PMF-cohort → ICP-criteria linkage (~30 min).
6. Skim [Wes Bush — *Product-Led Growth*](https://productled.com/book) Chapters 1-3 if selling into a PLG-shaped motion — this is the low-ACV persona-collapse case Chapter 4 discusses (~1 hr).
7. Skim [The Challenger Customer](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/315928/the-challenger-customer-by-brent-adamson-matthew-dixon-pat-spenner-and-nick-toman/) Introduction + Chapter on Mobilizers if selling at enterprise ACV (~1 hr).

Total: ~9 hours of prerequisite reading before authoring a defensible ICP scorecard against the exercises.

## Provenance

This module cites primary sources first (Moore's original books; Dunford's own writing; McMahon's practitioner text as MEDDIC co-creator; Rogers' original diffusion-of-innovations text; Ellis's original PMF-survey writing). MEDDIC / MEDDPICC / SPICED / Challenger references trace to the practitioners who originated or codified each acronym. Practitioner-writing links are included where they operationalise the primary sources cleanly and are broadly considered canonical within the early-stage GTM community. Data-source links (Sales Navigator, Crunchbase, BuiltWith, etc.) are the actual tools referenced in Chapter 2's firmographic-layer authoring discipline. If a factual claim in a chapter cannot be traced to one of the sources on this page (or to another primary source cited inline), it is a bug — please flag it via a `needs-research` comment against the offending chapter.
