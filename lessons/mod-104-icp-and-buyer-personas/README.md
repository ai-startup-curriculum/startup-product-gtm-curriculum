# mod-104 — ICP and Buyer Personas

**Stage:** SEED
**Pillar:** GTM
**Hours:** 22 (7 lecture chapters + 5 exercises + 1 lab)
**Track:** [Startup Product & GTM](../../CURRICULUM.md)

> *A first sales hire, six weeks in, should be able to qualify in or out on the first call — without escalating to the founder.*

## What this module trains

Author an **Ideal Customer Profile** precise enough for a first sales hire to run a 20-minute discovery call and end with a defensible in-or-out verdict — a two-layer scoreable filter (firmographic + behavioural criteria per [April Dunford — *Sales Pitch*](https://www.aprildunford.com/sales-pitch) and the practitioner ICP literature) plus a **disqualification checklist** that names *who is not the ICP* in the four archetypes (whole-product gap, ACV / budget-authority, pain-severity, beachhead-scope). Distinguish the **buyer** (signs), **user** (touches the product), and **champion** (advocates internally) as three separate personas — the split that becomes load-bearing at enterprise ACVs and is the site of MEDDIC / MEDDPICC's "Economic Buyer" failure mode. Score real inbound and outbound leads on **three independent axes** — ICP-fit, ACV-fit, timing-fit — and route by verdict (Priority 1 / Priority 2 / Nurture / Downgrade-or-Refer / Close-lost). Enforce [Geoffrey Moore — *Crossing the Chasm*](https://www.harpercollins.com/products/crossing-the-chasm-3rd-edition-geoffrey-a-moore) beachhead discipline at the ICP layer with an explicit **not-now list** naming the three segments deliberately excluded during the current beachhead. Ship a one-page ICP scorecard a first AE hire can carry, and diagnose **ICP drift** — the founder-led-sales pattern of taking any customer that will pay — with named signals and an enforce-or-revisit protocol.

The failure mode this module exists to catch: **the founder writes an ICP that everyone in the company nods at, and no one — least of all the first AE hire — can actually apply on a live call; pipeline is 3× the size it should be, close rate half what it should be, sales cycle length twice what it should be, and no one can name why.**

## Learning objectives

- Define an Ideal Customer Profile precise enough for a first sales hire to qualify in / out on the first call — company-level firmographic + behavioural criteria, plus a disqualification checklist that names *who is not the ICP*.
- Distinguish buyer persona (the person who signs) from user persona (the person who touches the product) from champion persona (the person who advocates internally) — and know why an ICP without all three fails at enterprise ACVs.
- Score real inbound / outbound leads against the ICP scorecard — five inbound + five outbound — and separate ICP-fit from ACV-fit from timing-fit.
- Use Moore's beachhead-segment logic to pick one segment to say yes to and three segments to say no to now — the said-no-to segments are re-evaluated in the mod-107 motion-design and mod-108 channel-selection modules.
- Author a one-page ICP scorecard + disqualification checklist a first AE hire can carry.
- Recognise ICP drift — the founder-led-sales pattern of taking any customer that will pay — and know when to enforce the ICP versus revisit it.
