# mod-105 — Founder-Led Sales (Kazanjy End-to-End, First Twenty Paying Customers)

**Stage:** SEED
**Pillar:** GTM
**Hours:** 26 (8 lecture chapters + 7 exercises + 1 lab)
**Track:** [Startup Product & GTM](../../CURRICULUM.md)

> *If the founder cannot close the first 20-30 customers by hand, no sales hire will close the next 200.*

## What this module trains

Run Peter Kazanjy's founding-sales stage model — **prospect → outreach → discovery → demo → proposal → close** — end-to-end for the first 20-30 paying customers, and produce the *pack of artifacts* (cold outreach sequence, discovery script, demo checklist, proposal template, next-step email templates, rate card, CRM stage definitions, first-hire readiness checklist) that the first AE hire inherits. Author and run discovery calls that find the pain, size the pain, and qualify the buyer using [Winning by Design's SPICED framework](https://winningbydesign.com/resources/) for mid-market ACV — Situation, Pain, Impact, Critical event, Decision — and upgrade to [MEDDIC / MEDDPICC](https://meddicc.com/) (Metrics, Economic buyer, Decision criteria, Decision process, Identify pain, Champion; +Paper process, +Competition) as ACV crosses the SMB threshold. Enforce **next-step discipline** on every call — every conversation ends with a scheduled next step or an explicit disqualification — and run CRM hygiene at founder-scale (stage per opportunity, next-step per opportunity, dollar-value per opportunity, honest commit / best-case / pipeline weekly forecast) even if the CRM is a Google Sheet with six columns. Recognise and rehearse the four founder-led-sales anti-patterns (deep-discounting to close, feature-promising, taking non-ICP deals to hit a number, saying yes to unsupported scope) with pre-loaded say-no scripts. Diagnose the five signals that say it is time for the first sales hire — repeatable discovery script, repeatable win pattern, repeatable loss pattern with disqualifier discipline, founder as measurable bottleneck, pack passes a substitution test — and stage the mod-110 hand-off with a 60-90 day AE ramp plan and a written founder-out plan.

The failure mode this module exists to catch: **the founder either refuses to sell — "I'm a builder, not a salesperson" — and hires a VP Sales in month 4 before personally closing anyone, then fires them in month 10 without a script to hand off; or sells opportunistically without a stage model, without CRM discipline, without a discovery script, and produces a pile of closed-won revenue with no repeatable pattern the first AE can inherit.** Both failure modes cost 12-18 months of runway and, empirically, the sales hire made without the founder having done the work.

## Learning objectives

- Run Kazanjy's founding-sales stage model end-to-end — cold outreach through discovery, demo, proposal, close, and next-step discipline — and produce the artifacts each stage requires (discovery call script, demo checklist, proposal template, next-step email).
- Author and run a discovery call — the *find the pain, size the pain, qualify the buyer* arc — using SPICED for mid-market and below, MEDDIC / MEDDPICC when ACV moves above the SMB range.
- Enforce next-step discipline on every call — every conversation ends with a scheduled next step or an explicit disqualification. No "let me follow up later" pipeline debt.
- Run CRM hygiene at founder-led-sales scale — stage per opportunity, next-step per opportunity, dollar-value per opportunity, honest weekly forecast — even in a spreadsheet.
- Close the first 20-30 paying customers by hand, recognise the founder-led-sales anti-patterns (deep-discounting to close, feature-promising, non-ICP deals, unsupported scope), and know when to say no.
- Diagnose the tell-tale signs it is time for the first sales hire — repeatable script, repeatable win-loss pattern, founder becoming the bottleneck — and set up the mod-110 first-hire staging.
- Ship a founder-led-sales pack — discovery call script, demo checklist, proposal template, next-step email templates, rate card, CRM stage definitions, and a first-hire readiness checklist.

## How this module connects to the rest of the track

- **Fed by ← [mod-101](../mod-101-customer-discovery-and-problem-validation)** — the discovery corpus and pain-shape synthesis are the raw material for the discovery script's Pain and Impact questions (Chapter 3) and the demo's pain-recap opening (Chapter 4). A discovery script authored without mod-101 draws its pain articulation from founder intuition and does not match how buyers actually describe the pain.
- **Fed by ← [mod-102](../mod-102-pmf-signal-and-measurement)** — the very-disappointed cohort's shared characteristics tell the founder whether a "yes" in discovery is a pull-signature yes or a polite-interest yes. Founder-led sales without mod-102 accumulates closed-won revenue without a durability signal, and the readiness diagnostic (Chapter 8) reads a false-positive win pattern.
- **Fed by ← [mod-103](../mod-103-positioning-and-messaging)** — the positioning statement (best-for characteristics, market frame, alternative comparisons) is the demo's opening frame and the outbound email's value hypothesis. A discovery-and-demo motion without mod-103 defaults to feature tours.
- **Fed by ← [mod-104](../mod-104-icp-and-buyer-personas)** — the ICP scorecard is the discovery call in motion. Chapter 3's discovery script derives its Layer-2 behavioural questions from mod-104's scorecard; Chapter 5's next-step / disqualification rule derives from mod-104's disqualifier list; Chapter 7's win/loss reason codes feed mod-104's drift diagnosis.
- **Feeds → [mod-106](../mod-106-pricing-and-packaging)** — the rate card (Chapter 6), the discount pattern log, and the exception log are the primary empirical inputs to pricing / packaging design. A pricing pack built without the founder-led-sales rate-card discipline is speculation.
- **Feeds → [mod-107](../mod-107-sales-motion-design)** — Kazanjy's six-stage model, the CRM stage definitions (Chapter 7), and the win / loss pattern (Chapter 8) are the primary input to sales motion design as the company scales past founder-led. mod-107 codifies the motion; mod-105 characterises it first.
- **Feeds → [mod-108](../mod-108-demand-generation-and-channels)** — the outbound sequence (Chapter 2), the reply-rate math, and the channel mix diagnosis are the empirical inputs to scaled demand generation. Founder-scale outbound is the pilot; mod-108 scales the pilot to a channel program.
- **Feeds → [mod-109](../mod-109-retention-expansion-and-growth-loops)** — the closed-won cohort produced by mod-105 is the primary retention / expansion opportunity mod-109 operates on. Poor founder-led-sales discipline (non-ICP deals, feature promises) upstream produces retention problems downstream.
- **Feeds → [mod-110](../mod-110-gtm-metrics-and-first-hires)** — the readiness diagnostic (Chapter 8), the pack, and the founder-out plan are the direct inputs to mod-110's first-hire compensation, rubric, ramp, and org design. Chapter 8's five signals are the SEED-to-Series-A transition trigger.
- **Feeds → [`project-101-first-thirty-paying-customers`](../../projects/project-101-first-thirty-paying-customers)** — the pack is the operating instrument the first-30-customer push runs on. Every stage exercise is a sub-pack for the corresponding stage of the project.
