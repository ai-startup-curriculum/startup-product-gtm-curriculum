# Resources — mod-109 Retention, Expansion, and Growth Loops

Citable references the module is grounded in. Primary sources first; adjacent practitioner writing second. Every chapter cites at least one item on this list.

## Cohort retention as the durability signal

- **Brian Balfour — "The Never Ending Road to Product/Market Fit".** Argues PMF is a system state, not a milestone; retention flattening is the durable signature. Load-bearing for Chapter 1. [https://brianbalfour.com/essays/product-market-fit](https://brianbalfour.com/essays/product-market-fit).
- **Brian Balfour — "Retention is King".** The canonical practitioner argument for retention as the compounding lever. [https://brianbalfour.com/essays/retention-engagement-growth](https://brianbalfour.com/essays/retention-engagement-growth).
- **Brian Balfour / Reforge — Four Fits framework.** Market / product / channel / model fit; the retention-as-durability read sits inside the Model-fit corner. [https://brianbalfour.com/four-fits-growth-framework](https://brianbalfour.com/four-fits-growth-framework).
- **Andrew Chen — "The Power User Curve".** Popularised the bimodal power-user distribution and the "smile" curve reading. [https://andrewchen.com/power-user-curve/](https://andrewchen.com/power-user-curve/); index of retention essays at [https://andrewchen.com/](https://andrewchen.com/).
- **Andrew Chen — "New data shows losing 80% of mobile users is normal".** Canonical practitioner benchmark reference for consumer mobile retention. [https://andrewchen.com/new-data-shows-why-losing-80-of-your-mobile-users-is-normal-and-that-the-best-apps-do-much-better/](https://andrewchen.com/new-data-shows-why-losing-80-of-your-mobile-users-is-normal-and-that-the-best-apps-do-much-better/).
- **Andrew Chen — *The Cold Start Problem* (2021).** Book-length treatment of network-effects products, including deep retention-curve reads and the activation vocabulary Chapter 5 leans on. [https://www.coldstart.com/](https://www.coldstart.com/).
- **Amplitude — "The Amplitude Guide to Retention".** Practitioner reference for cohort retention analytics setup and reading. [https://amplitude.com/blog/product-retention](https://amplitude.com/blog/product-retention).
- **Mixpanel — Retention analysis docs.** Reference for cohort retention reports and the definition tradeoffs. [https://docs.mixpanel.com/docs/reports/retention](https://docs.mixpanel.com/docs/reports/retention).
- **Reforge — blog writing on retention and growth.** Ongoing practitioner writing from Balfour's team. [https://www.reforge.com/blog](https://www.reforge.com/blog).

## GRR, NRR, and the SaaS metric canon

- **David Skok — "SaaS Metrics 2.0" (forEntrepreneurs).** The most-cited practitioner reference for SaaS metric definitions, including retention, expansion, and NRR. Load-bearing for Chapter 2. [https://www.forentrepreneurs.com/saas-metrics-2/](https://www.forentrepreneurs.com/saas-metrics-2/).
- **OpenView Partners — SaaS Benchmarks reports (annual).** Practitioner benchmark reports for NRR / GRR by ARR band, pricing model, and motion. [https://openviewpartners.com/expansion-saas-benchmarks/](https://openviewpartners.com/expansion-saas-benchmarks/); overview index at [https://openviewpartners.com/](https://openviewpartners.com/).
- **Bessemer Venture Partners — State of the Cloud / BVP Cloud Index.** Annual public-market SaaS metric reports; primary source for the "NRR ≥ 120% is the growth-phase archetype" claim. [https://www.bvp.com/atlas](https://www.bvp.com/atlas); [https://cloudindex.bvp.com/](https://cloudindex.bvp.com/).
- **KeyBanc Capital Markets — Annual SaaS Survey.** Practitioner benchmark survey covering retention, NRR, GRR, and expansion rates by revenue band. Search "KeyBanc SaaS Survey" for the most recent year.
- **SaaStr (Jason Lemkin) — writing on NRR, GRR, and durability.** Ongoing operational commentary on retention and expansion benchmarks. [https://www.saastr.com/](https://www.saastr.com/).
- **ChartMogul — SaaS metrics writing.** Practitioner definitions and benchmark commentary on NRR / GRR, cohort analysis, and churn. [https://chartmogul.com/blog/](https://chartmogul.com/blog/).
- **Public S-1s and 10-Ks** — Snowflake, Datadog, Zoom, Slack, HubSpot, MongoDB, and similar company filings are primary sources for NRR / GRR benchmarks in the public cohort. SEC EDGAR is the free source of truth: [https://www.sec.gov/edgar/searchedgar/companysearch](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/searchedgar/companysearch).

## Suggested reading order for this module

If starting cold:

1. Re-read [Brian Balfour — "Retention is King"](https://brianbalfour.com/essays/retention-engagement-growth) (~20 min).
2. Read [Brian Balfour — "The Never Ending Road to Product/Market Fit"](https://brianbalfour.com/essays/product-market-fit) (~20 min).
3. Skim [David Skok — "SaaS Metrics 2.0"](https://www.forentrepreneurs.com/saas-metrics-2/) for the retention + expansion sections (~30 min).
4. Read [Reforge — "Growth Loops are the New Funnels"](https://www.reforge.com/blog/growth-loops) end to end (~20 min).
5. Read [Andrew Chen — "The Power User Curve"](https://andrewchen.com/power-user-curve/) (~15 min) and [Andrew Chen — "New data shows losing 80% of mobile users is normal"](https://andrewchen.com/new-data-shows-why-losing-80-of-your-mobile-users-is-normal-and-that-the-best-apps-do-much-better/) (~15 min).
6. Skim [Amplitude — North Star Playbook](https://amplitude.com/north-star) for the north-star vs. leading-indicator vocabulary (~30 min).
7. Skim [OpenView — SaaS Benchmarks](https://openviewpartners.com/expansion-saas-benchmarks/) and [Bessemer — State of the Cloud](https://www.bvp.com/atlas) for current NRR / GRR benchmark bands (~30 min).
8. Skim [Stripe — Billing docs / Smart Retries](https://stripe.com/docs/billing/revenue-recovery) and [Recurly Research — payment recovery](https://recurly.com/research/) for the dunning-programme baseline (~30 min).

Total: ~3.5 hours of prerequisite reading before running the exercises against real (or realistic hypothetical) data.

## Provenance

This module cites primary sources first (Balfour's own essays, Chen's own writing, Skok's forEntrepreneurs canon, McClure's original AARRR post, Vohra's First Round essay, Bessemer's own cloud reports, OpenView's benchmark reports, Stripe / Recurly / Chargebee's own docs). Practitioner-writing links are included where they operationalise the primary source cleanly and are broadly considered canonical within the early-stage GTM community. No third-party summaries, no undated slide decks. Public S-1s / 10-Ks and SEC EDGAR are the free source of truth for the public-company NRR / GRR benchmarks in Chapter 2 and Chapter 4. If a factual claim in a chapter cannot be traced to one of the sources on this page (or to another primary source cited inline), it is a bug — please flag it via a `needs-research` comment against the offending chapter.
