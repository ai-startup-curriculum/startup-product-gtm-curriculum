# mod-110 — GTM Metrics and First Hires — CAC, LTV, Payback, and Staging the Team

**Stage:** SERIES-A
**Pillar:** GTM
**Hours:** 20 (7 lecture chapters + 6 exercises + 1 lab)
**Track:** [Startup Product & GTM](../../CURRICULUM.md)

> *The founder's operator numbers. What you read on Monday morning to run the week; what you show the board on Friday afternoon to defend the plan; the specific GTM signal that triggers each of the first three hires.*

## What this module trains

Take the retention read from [mod-109](../mod-109-retention-expansion-and-growth-loops) and the pricing / motion / demand-gen work from [mod-106](../mod-106-pricing-and-packaging), [mod-107](../mod-107-sales-motion-design), and [mod-108](../mod-108-demand-generation-and-channels), and turn them into the **operator numbers a founder or GTM lead uses to run the business** — **CAC (fully-loaded, per-channel)**, **LTV (cohort-based, not blended-ARR)**, **payback period**, **magic number**, **sales efficiency**, **funnel conversion**, **cohort retention**, and **NRR** — each read against **OpenView / SaaStr / KeyBanc benchmark bands by ARR band and motion type**. Distinguish this *operator view* from the *fundraising / capital-allocation deep view* that defers up to [`startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum). Author a **weekly + monthly GTM one-pager** the founder reads to run the week and that produces the GTM section of the board pack — with the discipline of separating **leading indicators** (activity, pipeline creation) from **lagging indicators** (revenue, retention, NRR). Then stage the **first three GTM hires**: founder-led sales through PMF; the first AE only after the founder can produce a repeatable script; the first product-marketing or demand-gen lead only after the first AE is closing at target; the first sales manager only after ≥3 AEs. Diagnose the **wrong-first-hire pattern** — a VP Sales hired to invent a motion the founder never validated, an SDR team stood up to feed an AE motion that has no repeatable AE — and prescribe the correct sequence.

The failure mode this module exists to catch: **the founder ships a Series-A plan built on a blended CAC that averages founder-outbound with paid, an LTV built on ARR ÷ blended-churn (not a cohort read), a payback quoted at the gross-margin-forgetting version, and then hires a VP Sales to "scale" a motion the founder never proved was repeatable — burning 12–18 months of runway and one senior GTM leader before discovering the numbers were wrong and the motion was not yet ready to hand off.**

The remedy this module ships: a **GTM operator one-pager** with the current week's numbers, decomposed per-channel and per-segment, benchmarked against OpenView / SaaStr / KeyBanc bands, with leading and lagging indicators separated; and a **12-month first-hire staging plan** with the specific GTM trigger criteria that qualify each hire (which numbers must be hit, which artifacts must exist, which failure modes must be ruled out) — such that a founder, an incoming VP GTM, or a Series-B underwriter can read the sequence and know why hire N is now and hire N+1 is not.

## Learning objectives

- Read the GTM operator numbers — CAC (fully-loaded, per-channel), LTV (cohort-based, not blended-ARR), payback period (months to recover CAC), magic number (net-new ARR ÷ prior-quarter S&M spend), sales efficiency, funnel conversion, cohort retention, NRR — against OpenView / SaaStr benchmarks by ARR band and motion type.
- Distinguish the founder's operator view of unit economics from the fundraising / capital-allocation deep view — this module covers the operator view; the deep modelling defers up to [`startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum).
- Author a weekly + monthly GTM metrics one-pager — the numbers a founder / GTM lead uses to run the week and the board pack that produces — and know which numbers are leading (activity, pipeline) vs. lagging (revenue, retention).
- Stage the first three GTM hires — founder-led sales through PMF, first AE after the founder can produce a repeatable script, first product marketer or demand-gen lead after the first AE is closing at target, first sales manager only after ≥3 AEs — and know the over-hire trap (a VP Sales before PMF, an SDR team before the AE motion works).
- Diagnose the wrong-first-hire pattern — hiring a VP Sales expecting them to invent a motion the founder never validated; hiring an SDR to feed an AE motion that has no repeatable AE — and prescribe the correct sequence.
- Ship a GTM one-pager (current-week numbers) + a first-hire staging plan for the next 12 months with trigger criteria per hire.
- Recognise the boundary — GTM-org hiring / comp / IC-plan depth defers to [`startup-operations-governance-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-operations-governance-curriculum); the *staging* logic (which hire when, based on what GTM signal) lives here.

## How this module connects to the rest of the track

- **Fed by ← [mod-102](../mod-102-pmf-signal-and-measurement)** — the PMF verdict is the primary trigger that qualifies "founder-led sales" to end and "first AE hire" to begin. Chapter 6's hire-staging logic depends on the mod-102 scorecard being green (or at least green in a specific ICP tier).
- **Fed by ← [mod-105](../mod-105-founder-led-sales)** — the *repeatable script*, *repeatable win-loss pattern*, and *founder-becoming-the-bottleneck* readiness signals authored in mod-105 Chapter 6 are the specific artifacts Chapter 6 of this module treats as the pre-conditions for the first AE hire.
- **Fed by ← [mod-106](../mod-106-pricing-and-packaging)** — pricing metric and packaging determine what the CAC / LTV / payback numbers can look like. A per-seat metric with strong seat-expansion supports different payback than a usage-metered infrastructure motion.
- **Fed by ← [mod-107](../mod-107-sales-motion-design)** — the sales motion type (PLG / SMB self-serve / mid-market AE / enterprise multi-threaded) determines the benchmark band the operator numbers must be read against. There is no single "SaaS payback benchmark"; there is a benchmark *per motion*.
- **Fed by ← [mod-108](../mod-108-demand-generation-and-channels)** — the per-channel CAC decomposition of Chapter 2 depends on mod-108's channel-portfolio work. A "blended CAC" that averages founder outbound with paid ads is the specific mistake this module trains you out of.
- **Fed by ← [mod-109](../mod-109-retention-expansion-and-growth-loops)** — GRR, NRR, cohort retention, and the expansion-vs-new-logo ARR split from mod-109 are core inputs to the one-pager. The cohort retention floor is what makes the cohort-based LTV in Chapter 3 defensible; a leaky floor invalidates the LTV number regardless of how carefully it is computed.
- **Feeds → [`project-103-pmf-and-retention-scorecard`](../../projects/project-103-pmf-and-retention-scorecard)** — the GTM operator one-pager is one of the three anchor artifacts this project packages (alongside the mod-102 PMF scorecard and the mod-109 retention + expansion scorecard). The first-hire staging plan is the project's sequencing spine.
