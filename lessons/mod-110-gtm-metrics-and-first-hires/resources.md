# Resources — mod-110 GTM Metrics and First Hires

Citable references the module is grounded in. Primary sources first; adjacent practitioner writing second. Every chapter cites at least one item on this list.

## The SaaS metric canon — CAC, LTV, payback, magic number, sales efficiency

- **David Skok — "SaaS Metrics 2.0" (forEntrepreneurs).** The most-cited practitioner reference for the SaaS metric definitions this module rests on. Load-bearing for Chapters 2, 3, and 4. [https://www.forentrepreneurs.com/saas-metrics-2/](https://www.forentrepreneurs.com/saas-metrics-2/).
- **David Skok — "LTV: What It Is and How to Calculate It" (forEntrepreneurs).** The canonical practitioner treatment of naïve vs. cohort-based LTV, gross-margin adjustment, and the horizon-cap discipline. Load-bearing for Chapter 3. [https://www.forentrepreneurs.com/ltv/](https://www.forentrepreneurs.com/ltv/).
- **David Skok — "Unlocking the Path to Negative Churn" and related essays.** Chapter 3 and Chapter 4 lean on Skok's treatment of retention as the LTV floor and expansion as the LTV amplifier. [https://www.forentrepreneurs.com/](https://www.forentrepreneurs.com/) (search "negative churn").
- **David Sacks / Craft Ventures — "The Burn Multiple".** The canonical formulation of burn multiple (net cash burn ÷ net-new ARR) as the operator efficiency metric that survives a downturn. Load-bearing for Chapter 4. [https://sacks.substack.com/](https://sacks.substack.com/) (search "burn multiple").
- **Scale Venture Partners — Scale Studio.** Practitioner reference for magic number, sales efficiency, and the SaaS efficiency canon. [https://www.scalevp.com/scale-studio/](https://www.scalevp.com/scale-studio/).
- **Meritech Capital — public-SaaS benchmarks.** Public-market SaaS efficiency reference for magic number, growth-adjusted efficiency, and rule-of-40. [https://www.meritechcapital.com/](https://www.meritechcapital.com/) and the periodic public-SaaS overview posts.

## SaaS benchmark reports — the bands the operator numbers read against

- **OpenView Partners — Expansion SaaS Benchmarks (annual).** Practitioner benchmark report for CAC, LTV, payback, NRR / GRR by ARR band, pricing model, and motion type. The primary source for Chapter 2, 3, and 4 benchmark call-outs. Cite the vintage year explicitly in one-pagers and board packs — the numbers shift year-over-year. [https://openviewpartners.com/expansion-saas-benchmarks/](https://openviewpartners.com/expansion-saas-benchmarks/); overview index at [https://openviewpartners.com/](https://openviewpartners.com/).
- **KeyBanc Capital Markets — Annual SaaS Survey.** Practitioner benchmark survey covering CAC, payback, NRR, GRR, magic number, and sales-efficiency ratios by revenue band. Search "KeyBanc SaaS Survey" for the most recent year.
- **Bessemer Venture Partners — State of the Cloud / BVP Cloud Index.** Annual public-market SaaS metric reports; primary source for the "NRR ≥ 120% is the growth-phase archetype" claim and for public-comparable magic-number ranges. [https://www.bvp.com/atlas](https://www.bvp.com/atlas); [https://cloudindex.bvp.com/](https://cloudindex.bvp.com/).
- **SaaStr — Jason Lemkin's writing on benchmarks and efficiency.** Ongoing operational commentary on CAC / payback / NRR / magic number ranges. [https://www.saastr.com/](https://www.saastr.com/).
- **ChartMogul — SaaS metric definitions and benchmark writing.** Practitioner reference for the metric definitions, retention decompositions, and benchmark commentary. [https://chartmogul.com/blog/](https://chartmogul.com/blog/).
- **Public S-1s and 10-Ks** — Snowflake, Datadog, HubSpot, Zoom, Slack, MongoDB, Twilio, Bill.com, and comparable public SaaS filings are the primary source for magic-number / NRR / payback benchmarks in the public cohort. SEC EDGAR is the free source of truth: [https://www.sec.gov/edgar/searchedgar/companysearch](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/searchedgar/companysearch).

## Staging the first GTM hires — the practitioner canon

- **Pete Kazanjy — *Founding Sales* (freely available online).** The definitive practitioner treatment of the founder-led-to-first-AE transition, the pre-conditions for the first AE hire, and the AE-first-then-SDR sequence. Load-bearing for Chapter 6 and Exercise 05. [https://foundingsales.com/](https://foundingsales.com/).
- **Mark Roberge — *The Sales Acceleration Formula* (2015).** HubSpot's early-sales-hiring playbook — the science-of-hiring approach, the specific competency profile (curiosity, coachability, work ethic, prior success, intelligence), the founder-to-first-AE handoff, and the ramp-plan discipline. Load-bearing for Chapter 6 and Exercise 05. [https://www.markroberge.com/](https://www.markroberge.com/).
- **Jason Lemkin — SaaStr writing on the first VP Sales hire and when NOT to hire one.** The canonical practitioner writing on the "VP Sales before PMF" over-hire trap (Chapter 6 Pattern 1). [https://www.saastr.com/](https://www.saastr.com/) (search "first VP Sales" / "when to hire VP Sales" / "VP Sales fired"). The paraphrase "if you're the CEO and you can't sell your own product, no VP Sales can save you" is Lemkin.
- **Winning by Design — Blueprints on GTM hire sequencing.** Practitioner reference for the founder-led → first AE → PMM/DG → second AE sequence and the SDR-couples-to-AE rule (Chapter 6 Pattern 2). [https://winningbydesign.com/](https://winningbydesign.com/).
- **Aaron Ross — *Predictable Revenue* (2011).** The original SDR-centric outbound playbook that Chapter 6 partially argues *against* at pre-PMF stages. Referenced for context on the SDR-first pattern this module warns about. [https://www.predictablerevenue.com/](https://www.predictablerevenue.com/).
- **David Sacks — writing on the sales-hiring sequence and the "second class of hires" trap.** Practitioner writing on how the sequencing of the first ten GTM hires determines the company's Series-A shape. [https://sacks.substack.com/](https://sacks.substack.com/).
- **First Round Review — GTM leadership hiring case studies.** Long-form pieces on VP Sales hires that went right and wrong; useful for Exercise 05's fourth-pattern authoring. [https://review.firstround.com/](https://review.firstround.com/) (search "VP Sales" / "sales hire").

## Suggested reading order for this module

If starting cold:

1. Read [David Skok — "SaaS Metrics 2.0"](https://www.forentrepreneurs.com/saas-metrics-2/) end to end (~30 min) — the definitional canon behind Chapters 2, 3, and 4.
2. Read [David Skok — "LTV: What It Is and How to Calculate It"](https://www.forentrepreneurs.com/ltv/) (~15 min) — the cohort-based LTV formulation Chapter 3 uses.
3. Read [David Sacks — "The Burn Multiple"](https://sacks.substack.com/) (~10 min) — the burn-multiple canon Chapter 4 references.
4. Skim [OpenView — Expansion SaaS Benchmarks (most recent vintage)](https://openviewpartners.com/expansion-saas-benchmarks/) (~20 min) — the benchmark bands the operator numbers read against.
5. Skim [KeyBanc SaaS Survey (most recent)](https://www.saastr.com/) (search "KeyBanc SaaS Survey") and [Bessemer — State of the Cloud](https://www.bvp.com/atlas) (~20 min each) for the public and private benchmark cross-check.
6. Read the Pete Kazanjy [*Founding Sales*](https://foundingsales.com/) chapters on the founder-to-first-AE handoff and the SDR-first pattern (~45 min) — the pre-condition and sequencing canon behind Chapter 6.
7. Skim [Mark Roberge — *The Sales Acceleration Formula*](https://www.markroberge.com/) (Chapters on hiring criteria and ramp plans, ~30 min) — the hire-criteria canon Chapter 6 references.
8. Skim [Jason Lemkin / SaaStr on the first VP Sales hire](https://www.saastr.com/) (search "first VP Sales" / "when to hire VP Sales", ~30 min) — the over-hire-trap canon behind Chapter 6 Pattern 1 and Exercise 05.
9. Skim [Winning by Design blueprints](https://winningbydesign.com/) on GTM sequencing and lagging vs. leading indicators (~15 min) — the sales-motion operating canon behind Chapters 4, 5, and 6.
10. Skim [Amplitude — North Star Playbook](https://amplitude.com/north-star) (~30 min) — the leading-vs-lagging vocabulary Chapter 5 and Exercise 03 use.

Total: ~4 hours of prerequisite reading before running the exercises against real (or realistic hypothetical) data.

## Provenance

This module cites primary sources first (Skok's forEntrepreneurs canon, Sacks's substack on burn multiple, Kazanjy's *Founding Sales*, Roberge's *Sales Acceleration Formula*, Lemkin's SaaStr writing, Bessemer's own cloud reports, OpenView's benchmark reports). Practitioner-writing links are included where they operationalise the primary source cleanly and are broadly considered canonical within the early-stage GTM community. No third-party summaries, no undated slide decks. Public S-1s / 10-Ks and SEC EDGAR are the free source of truth for the public-company magic-number / NRR / payback benchmarks referenced in Chapters 2, 3, and 4. Where a specific quantitative benchmark band is cited (payback < 12 months for SMB, LTV/CAC ≥ 3× as the working bar, burn multiple < 1× as best-in-class, and similar), the chapter includes a `<!-- needs-research: ... -->` marker calling out that the specific numeric threshold shifts year-over-year and should be pinned to the current-vintage source (OpenView / KeyBanc / Bessemer / SaaStr) at the time of the module's ship. If a factual claim in a chapter cannot be traced to one of the sources on this page (or to another primary source cited inline), it is a bug — please flag it via a `needs-research` comment against the offending chapter.
