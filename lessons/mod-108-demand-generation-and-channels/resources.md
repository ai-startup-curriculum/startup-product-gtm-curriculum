# Resources — mod-108 Demand Generation and Channels

Citable references the module is grounded in. Primary / foundational texts first; canonical practitioner writing second; platform documentation and tool references third. Every chapter cites at least one item on this list; every module-level claim about a named framework (Bullseye, Four-Fit, SPACES, Communities of Practice, Predictable Revenue cadence, HubSpot inbound methodology, direct-response discipline, law of shitty click-throughs) traces back to a primary source below.

## The channel-portfolio doctrine (Chapter 1)

- **Gabriel Weinberg and Justin Mares — *Traction: How Any Startup Can Achieve Explosive Customer Growth* (Portfolio, 2015).** The canonical practitioner text on channel selection at the pre-scale stage. Names nineteen distinct acquisition channels and prescribes the **Bullseye Framework** — brainstorm every channel, rank by plausibility, run three cheap experiments, double down on the single channel showing the strongest signal. Book site: [https://tractionbook.com/](https://tractionbook.com/).
- **Peter Thiel and Blake Masters — *Zero to One: Notes on Startups, or How to Build the Future* (Crown Business, 2014), Chapter 11 ("If You Build It, Will They Come?").** Thiel's investor-perspective version of the single-channel doctrine. The canonical line: *"If you can get just one distribution channel to work, you have a great business. If you try for several but don't nail one, you're finished."* Book site: [https://zerotoonebook.com/](https://zerotoonebook.com/).
- **Brian Balfour — the Four-Fit framework (2017).** The extension of product-market-fit into a four-layer dependency: product ↔ market, market ↔ channel, channel ↔ model, model ↔ market. The intellectual backbone of Chapter 2's diagnosis half. [Balfour — "The Never Ending Road To Product-Market Fit"](https://brianbalfour.com/four-fits-growth-framework); companion Reforge piece on growth loops: [Reforge — Four Fits / Growth Loops](https://www.reforge.com/blog/growth-loops).
- **Reforge — growth-model and growth-loop writing.** Long-running body of practitioner material on channel-market fit, growth loops, and portfolio discipline. [https://www.reforge.com/blog](https://www.reforge.com/blog).
- **Paul Graham — "Do Things That Don't Scale" (2013).** The founding-stage essay on the un-scaleable manual effort — hand-recruiting first users, hand-personalising outreach, hand-building the community — that precedes any scaled channel. The intellectual basis of the "founder-as-first-SDR" and "cohort-recruitment-is-named-and-personal" disciplines in Chapters 4 and 5. [https://paulgraham.com/ds.html](https://paulgraham.com/ds.html).

## Channel-market fit diagnosis (Chapter 2)

- **David Skok — "SaaS Metrics 2.0" (For Entrepreneurs).** The reference material on CAC, LTV, payback, and the unit-economics discipline that Chapter 2's Gate 2 depends on. [https://www.forentrepreneurs.com/saas-metrics-2/](https://www.forentrepreneurs.com/saas-metrics-2/).
- **Bessemer Venture Partners — State of the Cloud reports.** Long-running series of published benchmarks for B2B SaaS unit economics, including payback-period and CAC-ratio ranges by stage. [https://www.bvp.com/atlas](https://www.bvp.com/atlas).
- **SaaStr — Jason Lemkin's archive of SaaS unit-economics writing and benchmark posts.** [https://www.saastr.com/](https://www.saastr.com/).
- **OpenView Partners — SaaS benchmarks and the Expansion SaaS series.** Historical practitioner benchmarks for CAC, payback, and NRR by stage. Archive: [https://openviewpartners.com/blog/](https://openviewpartners.com/blog/). Note: OpenView wound down as a firm in 2024; the published research archive remains the canonical historical benchmark data. The successor practitioner source is Kyle Poyar's **Growth Unhinged** newsletter: [https://www.growthunhinged.com/](https://www.growthunhinged.com/).
- **First Round Review — long-form essays on early-stage GTM including channel diagnosis.** [https://review.firstround.com/](https://review.firstround.com/).

## Inbound / content + SEO (Chapter 3)

**The HubSpot inbound methodology — the vocabulary anchor.**

- **Brian Halligan and Dharmesh Shah — *Inbound Marketing: Get Found Using Google, Social Media, and Blogs* (Wiley, 2010; revised editions).** The founding text of the modern inbound-marketing methodology — attract / engage / delight (originally attract / convert / close / delight), the flywheel-vs-funnel framing, and the permission-based claim at the heart of inbound. Wiley: [https://www.wiley.com/en-us/Inbound+Marketing%2C+Revised+and+Updated%3A+Attract%2C+Engage%2C+and+Delight+Customers+Online-p-9781118896655](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/Inbound+Marketing%2C+Revised+and+Updated%3A+Attract%2C+Engage%2C+and+Delight+Customers+Online-p-9781118896655).
- **HubSpot — Inbound Methodology overview and HubSpot Academy certification.** The living documentation of the methodology; the Academy's Inbound Certification is the free practitioner course. [hubspot.com/inbound-marketing](https://www.hubspot.com/inbound-marketing); [HubSpot Academy — Inbound Certification](https://academy.hubspot.com/courses/inbound-certification).

**Modern SEO — Helpful Content, E-E-A-T, topical authority.**

- **Google Search Central — "Creating helpful, reliable, people-first content."** The canonical Google documentation on the Helpful Content update series and the E-E-A-T evaluation framework (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trust). [https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content); E-E-A-T anchor: [https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content#eeat](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content#eeat).
- **Google — Search Quality Rater Guidelines (publicly released PDF).** The guidelines Google's human raters use when evaluating search results; the operational definition of E-E-A-T in practice. Current PDF: [https://static.googleusercontent.com/media/guidelines.raterhub.com/en//searchqualityevaluatorguidelines.pdf](https://static.googleusercontent.com/media/guidelines.raterhub.com/en//searchqualityevaluatorguidelines.pdf).
- **Rand Fishkin — "Why Good Unique Content Needs to Die" (Moz, 2015).** The canonical essay on the 10× bar — do not publish a post unless it is 10× better than the currently top-ranked piece. The rule that Google's Helpful Content update later effectively encoded. [https://moz.com/rand/why-good-unique-content-needs-to-die/](https://moz.com/rand/why-good-unique-content-needs-to-die/).
- **Rand Fishkin — SparkToro and continued SEO writing.** The successor source to Fishkin's Moz-era work; audience-research and modern-SEO perspectives. [https://sparktoro.com/blog](https://sparktoro.com/blog).

**Content strategy — practitioner tradition.**

- **Joe Pulizzi — *Content Inc.* (McGraw-Hill, 2015; revised 2021).** The practitioner text on building a content programme as a business function. Content Marketing Institute (which Pulizzi founded) is the long-running trade organisation. [https://contentmarketinginstitute.com/](https://contentmarketinginstitute.com/).
- **Backlinko / Brian Dean — SEO case studies and ranking analyses.** Long-running archive of SEO practitioner material, including the published analyses on content length, backlink patterns, and ranking signals that practitioners cite for the compounding profile. [https://backlinko.com/](https://backlinko.com/).
- **Ahrefs blog — practitioner SEO writing, keyword research, and ranking-signal studies.** [https://ahrefs.com/blog](https://ahrefs.com/blog).
- **Semrush — practitioner SEO writing, topical-authority analyses, and research reports.** [https://www.semrush.com/blog/](https://www.semrush.com/blog/).

**LLM-cited-search shift.**

- **Google — AI Overviews / Search Generative Experience documentation.** Google's documentation of the AI-answer surface and its interaction with the organic SERP. [https://blog.google/products/search/generative-ai-search/](https://blog.google/products/search/generative-ai-search/).
- **Perplexity — citations and source documentation.** How Perplexity cites sources in its answers; relevant for authors optimising for LLM-cited-search. [https://docs.perplexity.ai/](https://docs.perplexity.ai/).

## Outbound at seed scale (Chapter 4)

- **Aaron Ross and Marylou Tyler — *Predictable Revenue: Turn Your Business Into A Sales Machine with the $100 Million Best Practices of Salesforce.com* (PebbleStorm, 2011).** The founding text of the modern outbound SDR-AE motion — role-split, cadence discipline, hand-off from SDR to AE. - **Sam Blond — The SaaStr podcast + practitioner writing (ex-Brex, ex-Zenefits).** Operator writing on outbound-at-scale and the SDR-AE motion. Writing archive: [https://www.saastr.com/author/samblond/](https://www.saastr.com/author/samblond/).
- **Kellen Casebeer — practitioner writing on cold outbound and signal-selection list-building.** [https://kellencasebeer.com/](https://kellencasebeer.com/).
- **Outreach — "State of Sales" reports.** Industry benchmark data on cadence structure, reply rates, and SDR-AE operating patterns. [https://www.outreach.io/resources](https://www.outreach.io/resources).
- **Salesloft — "State of Sales Engagement" research.** Companion benchmark data on cadence patterns and SDR productivity. [https://salesloft.com/resources/](https://salesloft.com/resources/).

## Community motion (Chapter 5)

**Academic foundation.**

- **Etienne Wenger — *Communities of Practice: Learning, Meaning, and Identity* (Cambridge University Press, 1998).** The academic foundation of the community-of-practice concept. The three defining features — **shared domain, community, practice** — are the test Chapter 5 uses to distinguish a community from a mailing list or support forum. Cambridge: [https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/communities-of-practice/728894B8653B73A0356F910BD9FD4B35](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/communities-of-practice/728894B8653B73A0356F910BD9FD4B35).
- **Etienne Wenger-Trayner — "Communities of Practice: A Brief Introduction."** The author's own one-page summary. [https://www.wenger-trayner.com/introduction-to-communities-of-practice/](https://www.wenger-trayner.com/introduction-to-communities-of-practice/).
- **Etienne Wenger, Richard McDermott, and William Snyder — *Cultivating Communities of Practice: A Guide to Managing Knowledge* (Harvard Business School Press, 2002).** The applied-practitioner companion to the 1998 academic text, oriented at organisations that want to seed and sustain communities of practice. HBR Store: [https://hbr.org/product/cultivating-communities-of-practice-a-guide-to-managing-knowledge/2245-HBK-ENG](https://hbr.org/product/cultivating-communities-of-practice-a-guide-to-managing-knowledge/2245-HBK-ENG).

**Modern community practitioner canon.**

- **David Spinks — *The Business of Belonging: How to Make Community your Competitive Advantage* (Wiley, 2021).** The modern practitioner text on community-as-business-function. The **SPACES model** — Support, Product, Acquisition, Contribution, Engagement, Success — is Chapter 5's load-bearing outcome taxonomy. Book site: [https://www.thebusinessofbelonging.com/](https://www.thebusinessofbelonging.com/).
- **CMX Hub (founded by David Spinks).** The community-of-practice-for-community-professionals; conferences, research reports, and a long archive of practitioner writing. [https://cmxhub.com/](https://cmxhub.com/).
- **Bailey Richardson, Kevin Huynh, and Kai Elmer Sotto — *Get Together: How to Build a Community With Your People* (Stripe Press, 2019).** The practitioner text on the *founding* stage of a community — the first 10, 100, 1000 members and the shift from founder-broadcast to community-generation. [https://gettogetherbook.com/](https://gettogetherbook.com/).
- **Rosie Sherry — Rosieland and Ministry of Testing archive.** Long-running practitioner writing from someone who built one of the earliest practitioner communities (Ministry of Testing) and now writes on community operating discipline. [https://rosie.land/](https://rosie.land/).

**Community-led growth as a GTM function.**

- **Reforge — Community-Led Growth course and writing.** Operator writing on community as a growth loop, especially the critical-mass and activation-energy conditions. [https://www.reforge.com/](https://www.reforge.com/).
- **Commsor and the broader community-ops tool ecosystem.** [https://www.commsor.com/](https://www.commsor.com/) (community operations platform; broader category includes Common Room, Orbit, etc.).

**Platforms for community (operational references).**

- **Slack** — [https://slack.com/](https://slack.com/) — default for small cohorts and existing-relationship chat.
- **Discord** — [https://discord.com/](https://discord.com/) — default for developer-adjacent communities and DevRel-anchored motions.
- **Discourse** — [https://www.discourse.org/](https://www.discourse.org/) — forum platform for content-community sub-motions where long-lived threads matter.

8. Read [Pete Kazanjy — *Founding Sales*](https://foundingsales.com/) end-to-end on founder-led sales and the hand-off to the first SDR (~3 hr).
9. Read [David Spinks — *The Business of Belonging*](https://www.thebusinessofbelonging.com/) Parts I-II on the SPACES model and the operating shape of community-as-business-function (~2 hr).
10. Skim [Etienne Wenger — "Communities of Practice: A Brief Introduction"](https://www.wenger-trayner.com/introduction-to-communities-of-practice/) for the three-feature test (~20 min).
11. Read [Bailey Richardson, Kevin Huynh, Kai Elmer Sotto — *Get Together*](https://gettogetherbook.com/) end-to-end on seeding the first 10-100 members (~2 hr).
12. Skim [Claude Hopkins — *Scientific Advertising*](https://scientificadvertising.com/) for the direct-response fundamentals (~1 hr).
13. Read [Andrew Chen — "The Law of Shitty Clickthroughs"](https://andrewchen.com/the-law-of-shitty-clickthroughs/) end-to-end (~15 min).
